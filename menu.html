Menu

Everything below is made to order. Start from a base, add what you want, and we will fold it in front of you.

How to build your own

1

Pick a base

Sweet crêpe, savoury crêpe, waffle or pancake stack. Sweet and savoury both start at €4.00.

2

Choose your fillings

Two included, then €0.60 – €1.20 per extra depending on the ingredient. Ask and we will price it before we pour.

3

Say how you want it

Folded, rolled, cut into rolls for sharing, or open on a plate with a fork. No extra charge either way.
