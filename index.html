Our story

Twenty-five years of getting one thing right.

Almyro Glyko opened in Nicosia in 1999 with a single griddle and a simple idea: a crêpe should be poured, filled and folded in front of you. We have not changed that. The batter is ours, the fillings are prepped fresh each morning, and nothing goes out that we would not eat ourselves.

The name says the rest. Almyró means savoury, glykó means sweet — and we never made anyone choose. Ham and halloumi at lunch, chocolate and banana at midnight, from the same pan.

Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Do you deliver?
Yes — through Wolt and Foody across Nicosia, and directly from the Omirou shop by phone. Delivery hours are shorter than shop hours, so check the app for your address.
Can I build my own crêpe?
That is the whole point. Start with a sweet or savoury base and add whatever you like from the counter — the price moves with the number of fillings, not with which ones you choose.
Do you have vegetarian options?
Plenty. Every sweet crêpe, waffle and pancake is vegetarian, and the savoury side has halloumi, mushroom, vegetable and cheese combinations. Tell us about allergies when you order and we will tell you honestly what we can do — we work on shared surfaces.
How far in advance should I book catering?
Two weeks is comfortable for a private party; a month or more for weddings and large corporate events, especially between May and September. Call the Omirou shop and ask for the events team.
Is there parking?
At The Mall of Cyprus, yes — free and covered. At Omirou you are in the city centre, so the Eleftheria Square and Solomos Square public car parks are your best bet, both a short walk away.
