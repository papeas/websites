Our story

It started with one bag that wouldn't wear out.

Anafi Bags began the way most small shops do — with a search for one good bag and a lot of disappointment along the way. Everything was either too precious to use or fell apart by winter.

So we started sourcing ourselves: a handful of makers, small runs, honest materials, and prices that don't pretend. Every piece we list is one we'd carry ourselves, and we say no to a lot more than we say yes to.

Materials & care

Look after it and it'll look after you

Leather

Wipe with a dry cloth, condition twice a year, and keep it out of direct sun. Scuffs settle into character.

Canvas

Spot-clean with mild soap and cold water. Air dry away from radiators — never in the machine.

Suede finish

Brush gently in one direction to lift the nap. Keep away from rain, or use a suede protector spray first.

Storage

Stuff with tissue so the shape holds, and store upright in the dust bag that came with it.

Good to know

Questions we get a lot

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same working day. Local delivery lands in 1–3 days; elsewhere it's usually 3–7 days and we'll send you a tracking number either way.

What does delivery cost?

A flat fee locally, and free on orders over €70. For anywhere else we quote you the exact cost in the DM before you pay anything.

Can I return or exchange a bag?

Yes — within 14 days, unused and in its dust bag. Exchanges are free; for refunds the return postage is on you. Just message us first so we can expect it.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, Revolut, or cash on delivery for local orders. We'll send the details once your piece is reserved.

Something sold out — will it come back?

Often, but not always: runs are small. Send us a message and we'll add you to the list for that piece and tell you the moment it lands.

Do you take custom or wholesale orders?

For larger orders and gifting, yes — get in touch and tell us the quantity and the date you need them by.
